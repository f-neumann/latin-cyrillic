Stroke recorder

Pick a letter, draw its strokes with the mouse (one drag = one stroke, in order), then copy the result into letters-data.js (uppercase/print) or cursive-letters-data.js (lowercase/cursive). The faded gray letter behind the canvas is a drawing template - either the current hand-authored version, or the real letterform from a Serbian cursive font, to trace over.

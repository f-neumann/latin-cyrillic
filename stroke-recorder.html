Stroke recorder

Pick a letter, draw its strokes with the mouse (one drag = one stroke, in order), then copy the result into letters-data.js (uppercase/print) or cursive-letters-data.js (lowercase/cursive). The faded gray letter behind the canvas is a drawing template - either the current hand-authored version, or the real letterform from a Serbian cursive font, to trace over. Or skip the freehand tracing: "Trace font shape" pulls the letterform's centerline straight out of the font image and offers it as one click-to-add candidate covering the whole letter (it doesn't know real pen order or direction yet - just the shape).
